# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- Compare contract relic rewards side by side on the game's relic cards. Select a card to confirm, or choose Later to keep the offer.

## 0.1.0

- Added optional weekly delivery contracts with coin payouts and a six-bread relic choice.
- Added controller-accessible offer, delivery and reward cards with native resource and reward icons. Contracts can be reopened from the calendar.
- Added checkpointed payments, reward recovery and support for kingdom resource equivalents, stock reservations and MoreRelics eligibility rules.
